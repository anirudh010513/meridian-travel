About

A small studio, deliberately

We plan a limited number of trips at a time. That is the only way the detail stays right and the person who planned your trip is the person who answers the phone.

Provisional identity. The name, marks and contact details on this site are placeholders for a demonstration. They are not a registered company, and nothing here should be read as a legal or corporate claim.

How a trip gets made

Step 1

Qualification

Dates, party, budget and pace are captured before anything is costed. If a trip cannot work in the budget, we say so early.

Step 2

Costing

Stays, transport and experiences are priced against real rates. Margin is checked against a floor before the itinerary is shown to you.

Step 3

Approval

A consultant signs off the quotation. A changed price becomes a new version with a note — never a silent edit.

Step 4

Holds and confirmations

Holds expire. Commits are approved individually against the specific amount. Nothing is bought twice: every booking carries a unique reference.

Step 5

On trip, and after

Day sheets, pickup confirmations and a daily check-in while you travel. Reconciliation closes the file after you return.

Principles

Four rules we do not trade away

No price without a person

A system can cost a trip. Only a consultant sends you the number.

No commitment without approval

Bookings, refunds and payouts are approved individually, against an amount and an expiry.

No silent edits

A changed itinerary is a new version with the difference stated. Old versions stay on file.

No invented facts

If we have not checked availability, we do not imply that we have. Indistinguishable from a booking is not a compliment.

Team, address and registrations

These are pending client confirmation. We have deliberately not invented staff names, awards, founding dates or registrations for this build.
