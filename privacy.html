Privacy & consent

What we collect, and why

Written in plain language. This is a demonstration build — the final statement must be reviewed by the client's legal adviser before launch.

Template notice. Company details, retention periods and the grievance contact are placeholders. Do not present this text as a legally reviewed policy.

What you give us

  • Your name, email address and — if you choose to give it — your phone number
  • Your trip details: destination, departure city, dates, party size, budget band and preferences
  • Any notes you add about the trip, including access or dietary requirements
  • A reference number we generate, so you can check progress

We do not ask for passport numbers, payment card details or government identifiers through this website.

Why we hold it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a costed itinerary
  • To operate the trip if you proceed — bookings, documents and support
  • To keep an accurate record of what was quoted, held, paid and refunded

We do not sell your data, and we do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing without a separate, explicit opt-in.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are written to our own travel platform database. Access is restricted to the consultant handling your enquiry and the operations team. Passport and identity documents, where they are collected later in the process, are restricted to the operations role and are never included in an itinerary, a customer message or a log line.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept while the enquiry is active and for a period afterwards for accounting and dispute purposes. Retention periods are to be confirmed with the client; this build does not implement automatic deletion.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry details at any time by replying to the consultant handling it, or by using the contact details on the contact page. If you withdraw consent before a quotation is issued, we will close the enquiry.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. The trip planner saves a draft of your in-progress form in your own browser so a refresh does not lose your answers; it stays on your device and is cleared when you submit.

Simulated content

Destination pages, journeys and prices on this build are sample content for demonstration. They are not offers, and no availability has been checked.
